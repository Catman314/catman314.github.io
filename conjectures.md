* Assuming the axiom of choice, Cesàro limits exist (see [the post]({% post_url 2025-07-30-cesaro-iteration %})).
* Absolutely convergent ordinal-indexed series can be rearranged.
* For cardinals $\kappa,\lambda$, if $2^{\kappa} = 2^{\lambda}$, then $\kappa = \lambda$.
  * I suspect that this is independent with ZFC. It is at least consistent because GCH is consistent.
* Is there a nontrivial analytic function $f(z) = \sum_{k=0}^\infty a_k z^k$ where the $a_k$ are rational and $f(n) = 0$ for all integers $n$?
  * There is $\sin(\pi z)$, but I couldn't find one with rational coefficients.
